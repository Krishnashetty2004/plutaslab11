The Manifesto

Why We Exist

We're a bunch of product makers figuring out our conviction.

Not everyone gets it. That's fine.

The best builders we know don't wait for permission. They ship. They learn by doing. They find their people — not in networking events, but in the trenches.

We wanted a space for that. A cozy place where we can lock in for 12 to 24 hours, build side by side, and learn from each other.

Plutas Lab is that space.

We're not an accelerator. We're not a co-working space. We're a community of builders who show up, ship, and help each other get better.

Some of us are working on startups. Some on side projects. Some are just exploring. All of us are building.

We're not for everyone. And that's the point.

If you love building more than talking about building — you might belong here.

Fit Check

We're Not For Everyone

Plutas Lab is for you if:

  • You're a product maker — you build things
  • You want to lock in and ship, not just talk
  • You want to learn from others who are also figuring it out
  • You're based in Bangalore (or willing to be here)
  • You're okay with late nights and early mornings

If this doesn't sound like you, that's totally fine. We're not trying to be for everyone.

The Vibes

What It's Like

This isn't a WeWork. It's not an office. It's not a networking event.

It's a cozy room full of builders doing their thing.

Late nights. Lo-fi beats. The sound of keyboards. Someone debugging at 2 AM. Someone else shipping their landing page. Chai runs. Sharing what you learned. Honest feedback.

FAQ

Questions

What exactly is Plutas Lab? +

A tech community of product makers based out of Bangalore HSR. We run Builders Night — 2-3 times a month, we lock in for 12-24 hours in our curated cozy space to build together and learn from each other. It's not a co-working space, not a hacker house, not an accelerator. It's a community for people who build.

What happens at Builders Night? +

You show up, lock in for 12-24 hours, and build. Work on your project, side project, or whatever you're exploring. Share what you're working on. Learn from others. Demo at the end if you want. It's low-pressure, high-focus time with other builders.

Is this a hacker house or residency? +

No. You don't live here. You come here to build. Then you go home.

How much does it cost? +

Builders Night is free. We're exploring ways to sustain this through sponsorships and grants.

Who is this for? +

Product makers. Builders. People who ship. Whether you're working on a startup, a side project, or just exploring — if you love building more than talking about building, you'll fit in. We're not for everyone, and that's intentional.

Do I need to be in Bangalore? +

Yes. This is fully in-person. We're based in HSR Layout, Bangalore. Remote doesn't work for what we're building.
